# User guide

This guide walks through running a Secret Santa with the app, from creating a group to everyone knowing who they're buying for.

## 1. Create a group

On the **Home** tab, tap **New group** and fill in:

- **Group name**, e.g. "Family Christmas 2026".
- **Your details**: your name, plus an optional phone and email. Your name appears at the bottom of every match message as the organizer. The app remembers these for your next group.
- **I'm taking part in the draw**: turn this on if you want a match too. Your own match is encoded like everyone else's, so you won't see it by accident. Turn it off if you're only organizing.

Tap **Create group**.

## 2. Add the event details

The **Details** tab holds:

| Field | Example |
| --- | --- |
| Budget | `$30` or `10 000 Ft` (free text) |
| Gift exchange date | Picked from a calendar |
| Location | Grandma's house |
| Notes | "Homemade gifts welcome!" |

These are included in every message. Changes save automatically.

The **Organizer** card at the top lets you edit your details or change whether you take part.

## 3. Add people

On the **People** tab, tap **Add person** for each participant.

- A **phone number** lets the app send their match by SMS.
- An **email** lets it send their match by email.
- With neither, you can still send it through any app (WhatsApp, Messenger, …) with the share sheet.

Names must be unique within a group, because they appear in messages.

## 4. Set rules (optional)

On the **Rules** tab, tap **Add rule** to stop someone from drawing a particular person.

- **Both ways** (the default): neither person can draw the other. Use this for couples.
- **One way**: only the first person is restricted.

Too many rules can make a draw impossible. If that happens, the app tells you which person to fix.

## 5. Draw names

Tap **Draw names**. You need at least 3 people. Once names are drawn, people and rules are locked so the draw stays consistent. To change them, use **Reset draw** (the ↺ button). Everyone then needs a new match.

## 6. Send the matches

The **Share matches** screen lists everyone. The pairings are hidden, so you can take part too.

- Tap **Send** next to a person to open SMS, email or the share sheet with their message already written. The ⋮ menu lets you choose a different way.
- A person is marked **Sent** when the app opens a messaging app for them. If you cancel the message, use ⋮ → **Mark as not sent**.
- The progress bar shows how many people have their match.

A message looks like this:

```
Hi Csani! 🎅

You're the Secret Santa for: [TcOhdMOp] 🎁

🔒 The name is encoded so nobody else can see it. Decode it in the
Secret Santa app's Decoder tab, or with any Base64 decoder.

🎄 Family Christmas 2026
💰 Budget: $30
📅 Gift exchange: December 24, 2026
...
```

Other options in the ⋮ menu at the top:

- **Reveal all pairings** shows every match on screen, after a warning. Only use it if you're not taking part, or don't mind spoiling your surprise.
- **Re-draw names** gives everyone a new match. Anyone you've already messaged needs their new one.

## 7. Decode a match

Anyone with the app can open the **Decoder** tab and:

1. Paste the code (`TcOhdMOp`) or the **whole message**. The app finds the code in the brackets.
2. When "Code found!" appears, make sure nobody is looking, then tap to reveal the name.

People without the app can paste the code into any Base64 decoder website.

> **Note:** Base64 hides the name; it doesn't encrypt it. It stops anyone from reading the name at a glance, for example the organizer while sending, but anyone who deliberately decodes the code can read it.

## Settings

Open **Settings** with the ⚙ button on the Home tab:

- **Colors**: Festive (red, green, gold) or Material You (from your wallpaper, Android 12+).
- **Appearance**: follow the system, light or dark.
- **Snowfall** on the Home tab.
- **About**: app version, source code and license.

## Your data

Everything is stored only on your phone. The app has no account, sends nothing to the internet, and asks for no permissions. Uninstalling the app deletes your groups.
